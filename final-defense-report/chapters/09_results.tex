\section{ML Model Performance}

\subsection{SLA Risk Model}

The GradientBoostingRegressor achieves strong predictive performance on the synthetic dataset:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{SLA Risk Model Metrics}
\label{tab:sla-metrics}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Train} & \textbf{Test} \\
\midrule
Mean Absolute Error (MAE) & $< 0.05$ & $< 0.06$ \\
Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) & $< 0.07$ & $< 0.08$ \\
$R^2$ Score & $> 0.90$ & $> 0.85$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Key observations:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Small train/test gap indicates low overfitting
    \item Cross-validation confirms stability across folds
    \item Feature importance analysis confirms latency and packet loss as top risk drivers
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Anomaly Detection Models}

Both IsolationForest models demonstrate effective anomaly separation:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{OSS Anomaly Model}: Successfully identifies network degradation patterns (throughput collapse, latency spikes, signal loss)
    \item \textbf{BSS Revenue Anomaly Model}: Detects SIM box fraud, dormant SIMs, and SMS spam patterns with high precision on extreme anomalies
    \item Contamination rate of 5\% matches expected real-world telecom fault frequencies
\end{itemize}

\section{Security Audit}

The platform satisfies security requirements:
\begin{itemize}
    \item JWT authentication on all API endpoints
    \item Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 12)
    \item No hardcoded credentials (environment variables only)
    \item pip-audit and safety dependency scanning in CI/CD
    \item OAuth2 social login for Google and GitHub
\end{itemize}
